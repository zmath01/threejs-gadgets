How this works

Each round shows one target figure, built from unit blocks. Study it — drag it the way you'd turn an object over in your hands, or walk around it in a room. Then look at the four candidates: exactly one is the same figure seen from a different angle. The rest are mirror images or altogether different figures. Pick the true rotation. Recognizing shape from any external point of view, not just your own, is the allocentric skill this drill builds.
